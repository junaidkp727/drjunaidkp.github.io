Profile Summary

Research Scientist at the HTA Resource Hub, IIPHG, with prior experience as Senior Research Fellow (Health Economics) at PGIMER, Chandigarh. My research focuses on Health Economics, HTA, and Mental Health Economics, combining positive health economics (trend analysis, hypothesis generation, prediction) with normative approaches, including economic evaluation and decision-analytic modelling. I have strong expertise in quantitative research methods, systematic reviews and meta-analysis, econometric and structural equation modelling, spatial analysis, and machine-learning-based prediction, with advanced proficiency in R, Stata, Python, SPSS, and GIS tools. I have published in indexed journals, contributed to funded grants (DHR, SERB, SERB-MATRICS), and delivered teaching and workshops in health economics and research methods.
